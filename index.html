My process

How I pick what I recommend

Affiliate buttons don't change the criteria. If a product can't pass all three steps, it doesn't make the page.

1

Deep research first

I dig through owner reviews, spec sheets and return-rate chatter across forums before anything gets shortlisted — the failure stories matter more than the marketing.

2

Weeks of real use

Shortlisted gear lives on my desk for at least two weeks of real work — calls, deep-focus sessions, the 3pm slump. Comfort failures only show up on day nine.

3

The buy-it-twice test

Would I spend my own money on it again, at full price? If the honest answer is no, it gets cut — no matter how good the commission is.
